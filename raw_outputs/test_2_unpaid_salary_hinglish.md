Main Gurgaon ki ek marketing agency mein social media executive tha. ## Unedited Model Output
Namaste, main NyayaSahayak hoon. Aapki samasya samajh aayi—Gurgaon ki marketing agency mein kaam karte hue resignation ke baad ₹56,000 (August aur September ka salary) roka gaya hai. Aapke paas acche proofs hain. 

Aapke paas **Payment of Wages Act, 1936 (Sections 4 & 15)** aur **Indian Contract Act, 1872** ke antargat kanooni vikalp uplabdh hain. 

### Aapke Paas Maujood Proofs:
Aapke paas jo proofs hain, woh is prakar hain (Verified Knowledge Base ke anusaar):
* Offer letter / employment contract aur salary slips
* Bank statements jismein past salary credits aur missing mahine dikhte hain
* Emails/WhatsApp messages jo aapne HR/management ko pending salary ke liye kiye hain
* Relieving letter ya attendance/work logs

### Step-by-Step Options (Kanooni Kadam):

1. **Formal Written Demand:** Ek formal written demand email HR/management ko bhejiye jismein exact unpaid months aur amount (₹56,000) ka spasht zikr ho.
2. **Legal Notice:** Ek formal Legal Notice Speed Post ya email ke madhyam se bhejiye jismein 15 din ka samay diya jaye ki aapka dues clear kiya jaye.
3. **Labour Commissioner / Civil Suit:** Agar aap Payment of Wages Act ke statutory wage bracket ke antargat aate hain, toh local Assistant/District Labour Commissioner ke paas complaint file kar sakte hain. Agar aapka salary bracket usse upar hai, toh Indian Contract Act, 1872 ke tahat civil recovery suit ya breach of contract ka rukh karna pad sakta hai.

### Important Warning (Cash/Proof Risk & Lawyer Check):
Kyunki aapne bataya hai ki aapke paas emails, salary slips, bank statements aur offer letter maujood hain, yeh ek majboot sthiti hai. Lekin agar kisi hisse ka payment cash mein bina kisi receipt ya written record ke tha, toh usko sabit karna mushkil hota hai. 

**Kab ek real lawyer se milein:**
Agar aap kisi managerial ya high-salary role par the jo Labour Commissioner ke jurisdiction mein nahi aata, ya fir agar company band hone wali hai/insolvent ho rahi hai, toh turant kisi real lawyer ki madad lein.
